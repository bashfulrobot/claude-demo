# Pick your skill

Decide what your skill *does* in one sentence before you scaffold. Keep it narrow: one trigger, one job, one named output.

Need a seed? Pick one and make it yours, or invent your own:

- Turn a call transcript into a follow-up email
- Slack thread → draft Salesforce case
- Weekly CSAT note from the raw numbers
- A requirements blob → a structured spec

!!! warning "Original only"
    Don't build the same skill as your neighbour. Choose something *you* keep doing by hand.

Got it in a sentence? Scaffold it.
